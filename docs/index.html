AI that runs on your phone.

whereismyai downloads open-source AI models from Hugging Face and runs them entirely on your device. No account, no server, no tracking — and once a model is downloaded, no internet connection either.

Source on GitHub Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Runs offline

Download a model once, then chat with no network at all.

Nothing leaves the device

There is no whereismyai server. Your chats are stored only on your phone.

Your choice of model

13 curated models, plus search across GGUF models on Hugging Face.

Sees images too

Vision models can answer questions about a photo you pick or capture.

Tunable

Per-model system prompt, sampling settings and context size.

Open source

Apache 2.0. Read the code and verify the privacy claims yourself.

Privacy in one line

The only server the app ever contacts is Hugging Face, and only when you search for or download a model. Everything else happens on your device.
