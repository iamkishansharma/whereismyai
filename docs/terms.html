ABOUT WHEREISMYAI

whereismyai is a free, open-source mobile application that allows you to download and run supported open-source AI models directly on your own device.

The App is designed to provide local AI features, including:

  • Downloading supported models from available model sources.
  • Running supported GGUF models on-device.
  • Text conversations with locally running models.
  • Image and vision-model interactions where supported.
  • Multiple conversations and conversation history.
  • Model-specific generation settings.
  • Local storage of conversations, models, attachments, and application preferences.
  • Offline AI use after required models and resources have been downloaded.

The App is provided free of charge. There is no subscription, no purchase, no account, and no whereismyai server.
